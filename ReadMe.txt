# US Auto Insurance Requirements

A free reference tool for researching automobile insurance requirements across all 50 U.S. states.

This website provides state-by-state information gathered from statutes, administrative regulations, insurance department publications, and other primary legal sources.

## Included Information

### Minimum Insurance Requirements

State-specific requirements may include:

* Bodily Injury (BI)
* Property Damage (PD)
* Personal Injury Protection (PIP)
* Uninsured Motorist (UM)
* Underinsured Motorist (UIM)

### Statutes of Limitation

Where available, the site includes:

* Bodily Injury limitation periods
* Property Damage limitation periods

### Policy Disclosure Requirements

Where applicable, the site includes:

* Whether disclosure is required
* Eligible requestors
* Disclosure deadlines
* Required disclosure contents
* Citation and source information

### Claims Handling Deadlines

Where applicable, the site includes:

* Claim acknowledgment requirements
* Investigation requirements
* Payment requirements
* Applicable citations and sources

## How To Use

### Search

Use the search box to find:

* States
* Coverage types
* Notes
* Legal references

### State Selection

Choose a state from the dropdown menu to view its information.

### Sources

Whenever available, source links are provided directly to the applicable statute, regulation, administrative code, or insurance department publication.

Users should always review the cited authority directly before relying on any information.

## Data Sources

This project prioritizes:

1. State statutes
2. Administrative regulations and codes

Whenever possible, direct links to the underlying authority are provided.
