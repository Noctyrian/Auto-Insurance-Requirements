# US Auto Insurance Requirements

A free, state-by-state reference for automobile insurance requirements, policy disclosure laws, claims handling deadlines, and related insurance information across all 50 U.S. states.

## Features

### Minimum Insurance Requirements

View state-specific requirements for:

- Bodily Injury (BI)
- Property Damage (PD)
- Personal Injury Protection (PIP)
- Uninsured Motorist (UM)
- Underinsured Motorist (UIM)

### Statutes of Limitation

Research limitation periods for:

- Bodily Injury claims
- Property Damage claims

### Policy Disclosure Requirements

Review:

- Disclosure eligibility
- Required contents
- Deadlines
- Affidavit requirements
- Citation sources

### Claims Handling Deadlines

Research:

- Acknowledgment requirements
- Investigation requirements
- Payment requirements
- Applicable legal authority

### Source Citations

Whenever available, information is linked directly to:

- State statutes
- Administrative codes
- Insurance department publications
- Other official government sources
